# Model 2 Operation and Maintenance Manual

## Overview

This manual covers operation and routine maintenance of **model2** equipment. The factory fleet includes 17 model2 machines, ranging in age from 2 to 20 years, with a mean age of 12.8 years. Use the sensor ranges and component replacement intervals below as the reference for routine checks and maintenance planning.

Operators should monitor the volt, rotate, pressure, and vibration readings during normal operation. Compare readings with the stated warning and critical thresholds, and report sustained abnormal readings for inspection. This guidance is intended for internal training and equipment reference; follow applicable site procedures when servicing a machine.

## Specifications

model2 is monitored through four sensor channels: electrical voltage, rotation speed, hydraulic pressure, and vibration. Each channel has a normal mean and threshold values for identifying readings that need attention. Voltage and pressure have both high and low thresholds; rotate and vibration have high thresholds only in the supplied data.

The serviceable components listed for model2 are the Spindle bearing, Hydraulic pump, Drive motor, and Coolant valve. Their recommended replacement intervals are given in days in the maintenance table. Use the component names as listed when recording inspection findings or planning replacement work.

## Normal operating ranges

| Sensor | Unit | Normal mean | Warning threshold | Critical threshold |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| volt | V | 170.8 | Low: 132.0; high: 209.6 | Low: 116.5; high: 225.1 |
| rotate | rpm | 446.6 | High: 578.3; low: not specified | High: 631.0; low: not specified |
| pressure | bar | 100.9 | Low: 73.2; high: 128.5 | Low: 62.2; high: 139.5 |
| vibration | mm/s | 40.4 | High: 53.8; low: not specified | High: 59.2; low: not specified |

A reading that reaches a warning threshold should prompt an operator check and be documented. A reading at a critical threshold should be escalated for prompt assessment under site procedures. “Not specified” indicates that no threshold in that direction was provided for the sensor.

## Maintenance intervals

| Component | Recommended replacement interval (days) |
|---|---:|
| Spindle bearing | 180 |
| Hydraulic pump | 150 |
| Drive motor | 210 |
| Coolant valve | 120 |

Track service history by component and use the listed interval when planning replacement. An abnormal condition may warrant inspection before the scheduled replacement interval.

## Common issues

- **Rising vibration:** Inspect the **Spindle bearing** for wear or damage. Compare the vibration reading with its warning and critical thresholds, and document any change in operating behavior.
- **Unstable or abnormal pressure:** Check the **Hydraulic pump** and its connections for signs of deterioration or impaired operation. Escalate pressure readings that reach a critical threshold.
- **Abnormal rotation or voltage readings:** Inspect the **Drive motor** and associated electrical connections. Record whether the reading is above or below its applicable threshold.
- **Inconsistent coolant delivery:** Inspect the **Coolant valve** for sticking, obstruction, or leakage. Report persistent delivery problems and arrange service according to site procedures.
