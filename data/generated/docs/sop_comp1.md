# SOP: Replacing the spindle bearing

## Scope

This procedure covers replacement of the **Spindle bearing** on model1, model2, model3, and model4. It is intended for trained maintenance personnel using the applicable model-specific service instructions. Replace the Spindle bearing at the recommended interval of 180 days, or sooner if inspection or operating symptoms indicate a problem. Confirm the correct bearing part number and any model-specific fit, preload, lubrication, and fastener requirements before starting. Do not substitute dimensions, clearances, torque values, or installation methods between models.

## Safety

Before any work on the Spindle bearing:
- Stop the machine and apply lockout/tagout to all electrical and other hazardous energy sources. Verify zero energy using the site procedure.
- Allow the spindle and nearby components to stop and cool. Secure the spindle against rotation and prevent movement of any raised or removable assemblies.
- Identify and safely release stored energy, including spring force, trapped pressure, or tension in associated components, following the model-specific service instructions.
- Wear safety glasses, cut-resistant work gloves when handling parts, and safety footwear. Use hearing protection or other PPE required by the work area.
- Use approved lifting equipment and team-lifting practices for heavy spindle assemblies. Keep hands clear of press points and suspended loads.
- Keep bearing surfaces clean; protect the Spindle bearing and open housing from dust, chips, and moisture. Handle any lubricant according to its safety data sheet.

Do not use impact tools or improvised methods to remove or install the Spindle bearing. Stop and consult a supervisor if the assembly, required tooling, or safe energy-isolation points are unclear.

## Tools and parts

Prepare the correct replacement Spindle bearing and any seals, retainers, or single-use fasteners specified for the model. Have the current service instructions, lockout/tagout equipment, hand tools, bearing puller or press with suitable adapters, approved lifting equipment, cleaning materials, and specified lubricant available. Use measuring and inspection tools required by the service instructions. Confirm that tools and adapters contact only the approved bearing surfaces; a press load applied through the rolling elements can damage the Spindle bearing.

## Replacement procedure

1. Confirm the machine model and identify the correct Spindle bearing part number using the parts information. Review the applicable service instructions and replacement interval.
2. Notify affected personnel, stop the machine, apply lockout/tagout, and verify zero energy. Complete the safety checks above before disassembly.
3. Clean the spindle area to limit contamination. Mark or document the position and orientation of covers, retainers, spacers, and related components as permitted by the service instructions.
4. Remove guards and components necessary to access the Spindle bearing. Support the spindle assembly with approved lifting equipment where required; do not load cables, seals, or unsupported parts.
5. Remove the specified retainers and extract the old Spindle bearing using the approved puller or press setup. Keep the tool aligned with the bearing seat and follow model-specific instructions for force direction.
6. Inspect the spindle shaft, housing, seats, and adjacent parts for wear, scoring, corrosion, looseness, or contamination. Stop for assessment if damage is found; do not install a new Spindle bearing into a damaged seat.
7. Clean and prepare the bearing seats using approved materials. Verify the replacement Spindle bearing matches the identified part and check its condition before installation.
8. Install the Spindle bearing with the specified orientation, fit, lubricant, and method. Use the approved adapter and apply installation force only to the race specified by the service instructions.
9. Reinstall retainers, seals, spacers, and removed components in their documented positions. Apply specified fastener requirements and confirm that the spindle turns as directed by the service instructions.
10. Remove tools and materials, restore guards, and account for personnel. Keep lockout/tagout in place until the verification and restart checks are ready to be performed under the site procedure.

## Verification and restart

Check that the Spindle bearing is seated and retained as specified, and that all guards and connections are restored. Clear the work area, notify affected personnel, and remove lockout/tagout according to site rules. Restart using the model-specific procedure, initially at the prescribed safe operating condition. Listen for abnormal noise and check for unusual vibration, heat, or looseness using approved monitoring methods. Stop and isolate the machine if an abnormal condition occurs. Record the replacement date, model, bearing part number, and inspection or restart findings.

## Time estimate

Allow **4 hours** for the Spindle bearing replacement, including access, removal, installation, and verification. Actual time may vary with model configuration, access, and inspection findings.
