# SOP: Replacing the drive motor

## Scope

This procedure covers removal, replacement, and restart checks for the **Drive motor** on models model1, model2, model3, and model4. Confirm the machine model and verify that the replacement Drive motor is approved for that model before beginning. The recommended replacement interval is 210 days; inspect operating history and maintenance records to confirm whether replacement is due. This procedure does not replace model-specific manufacturer instructions for wiring, mounting, alignment, or fastener torque. Use the applicable machine documentation wherever it specifies a different requirement.

## Safety

Before any work on the Drive motor:
- Stop the machine using its normal shutdown procedure, then isolate and lock out/tag out every electrical supply that can energize the Drive motor or connected equipment.
- Verify zero energy with an approved tester using the site’s test-before-and-after method. Allow capacitors to discharge as specified by the machine documentation, and verify they are discharged before touching electrical connections.
- Identify and control stored mechanical energy, including rotating parts, belt or coupling tension, gravity-loaded assemblies, and any attached equipment that could move. Secure or support components before loosening mounts.
- Wear safety glasses, suitable work gloves, and safety footwear. Use arc-rated PPE where the electrical risk assessment or site rules require it.
- Keep the work area dry and clear. Do not handle wiring or open an electrical enclosure until isolation has been verified.

Do not bypass guards or interlocks. If wiring, mounting, or stored-energy conditions cannot be safely identified, stop and consult a qualified technician.

## Tools and parts

Prepare the approved replacement Drive motor, machine-specific wiring and mounting documentation, insulated hand tools, a calibrated torque wrench, an approved electrical tester, and suitable lifting or supporting equipment if the motor’s weight or position requires it. Have labels or tags for identifying conductors, a camera or log sheet for recording the original connections, and replacement mounting hardware only if specified by the manufacturer. Use the prescribed alignment tools for the motor’s coupling or belt drive. Confirm that any lifting equipment is rated for the load.

## Replacement procedure

1. Confirm the machine model, replacement Drive motor part number, and applicable wiring and mounting instructions. Record the work order and existing fault or maintenance observations.
2. Shut down the machine, isolate all relevant supplies, apply lockout/tagout, and complete the safety checks above. Verify the Drive motor cannot start.
3. Remove guards or access panels needed to reach the Drive motor, keeping fasteners organized. Photograph the installation and label each conductor before disconnecting it.
4. Identify and safely release mechanical load on the Drive motor. Support the motor and any connected assembly before loosening mounts or transmission components.
5. Disconnect the Drive motor conductors according to the machine documentation. Protect cable ends from damage and contamination; do not pull on conductors to remove them.
6. Disconnect the coupling, belt, or other drive connection as applicable. Remove the mounting fasteners and lift the Drive motor clear using safe handling practices.
7. Inspect the mounting surface, coupling or belt components, and accessible cables for damage or wear. Report defects; do not install the replacement Drive motor onto an unsafe or damaged interface.
8. Position the replacement Drive motor in the original orientation. Install the specified mounting hardware and tighten to the documented torque. Reconnect the drive connection and align it using the manufacturer’s method.
9. Reconnect conductors to their labeled terminals using the wiring diagram. Check that connections are secure, cables are routed clear of moving or hot parts, and grounding is restored as specified.
10. Reinstall guards and panels. Account for tools and loose parts, then have a second qualified person review the installation where site rules require it.

## Verification and restart

Keep personnel clear, then remove lockout/tagout only under the site procedure and by the authorized workers who applied it. Restore power and perform the machine’s approved startup checks. Verify the Drive motor starts and stops normally, runs without unusual noise, vibration, odor, or overheating, and drives the connected equipment correctly. Confirm guards and interlocks function, then record the replacement date, motor identification, checks performed, and any defects or follow-up actions. Stop and isolate the machine if any abnormal condition occurs.

## Time estimate

Allow **6 hours** for the replacement, including isolation, removal, installation, alignment, and verification. Actual duration may vary with access, model-specific requirements, and any defects found.
