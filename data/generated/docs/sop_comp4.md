# SOP: Replacing the coolant valve

## Scope
This procedure covers replacement of the Coolant valve on model1, model2, model3, and model4. Replace the Coolant valve if it is damaged, leaking, or otherwise confirmed to require replacement. The recommended replacement interval is 120 days; follow the site maintenance schedule and equipment-specific instructions when they differ. Confirm that the replacement part is suitable for the machine before beginning.

## Safety
Before any work on the Coolant valve:
- Stop the machine and apply lockout/tagout to its electrical and other applicable energy sources. Verify isolation before touching the Coolant valve.
- Allow moving parts to stop and relieve or isolate pressure in the coolant circuit using the machine’s approved procedure. Do not loosen a connection while the circuit may be pressurized.
- Wear safety glasses or goggles, coolant-compatible gloves, and protective work clothing. Use additional PPE required by the coolant safety data sheet.
- Let hot coolant cool before handling. Drain or capture coolant in a suitable container, and prevent spills from reaching walkways or drains.
- Follow site procedures for coolant exposure, spill cleanup, and disposal. Do not mix or return contaminated coolant to the system.

## Tools and parts
Prepare a compatible replacement Coolant valve, the machine’s approved coolant catch container, absorbent materials, and any seals or fittings specified for the replacement part. Have the hand tools needed for the installed connections, a suitable method for cleaning and drying the work area, and lockout/tagout equipment available. Consult the applicable machine instructions for connection details, coolant handling requirements, and any specified tightening values; do not substitute guessed values.

## Replacement procedure
1. Confirm the machine model and identify the installed Coolant valve using the machine documentation. Verify the replacement Coolant valve and any required seals or fittings are correct.
2. Notify affected personnel, stop the machine normally, and isolate it using the site lockout/tagout procedure. Verify the machine cannot start.
3. Allow hot components and coolant to cool. Isolate the coolant supply and relieve circuit pressure according to the machine instructions.
4. Position the catch container beneath the Coolant valve. Drain or capture coolant as directed, and protect nearby electrical equipment and surfaces from spills.
5. Note or photograph the Coolant valve orientation and the location of connected lines or wiring, if present. Label connections where needed to support correct reassembly.
6. Using suitable tools, disconnect the lines and any other connections from the Coolant valve. Cap or protect open lines to limit contamination and leakage.
7. Remove the old Coolant valve without damaging adjacent components or connection surfaces. Check the mounting area and connected lines for visible damage, residue, or contamination.
8. Prepare the replacement Coolant valve and install specified new seals or fittings as required. Position it in the documented orientation and secure its connections according to machine instructions.
9. Reconnect lines and any other connections, checking that each is correctly routed and secured. Remove caps and account for tools, absorbents, and loose parts.
10. Clean any spilled coolant and dispose of captured coolant and waste under site procedures. Keep the machine isolated until the verification checks are ready.

## Verification and restart
With guards and access panels restored, clear personnel and tools from the work area. Follow the machine procedure to restore coolant and inspect the Coolant valve and its connections for leakage. If the system requires priming or bleeding, use the equipment-specific method. Restart only after the checks pass; observe the Coolant valve during initial operation for leaks or abnormal behavior. Stop and isolate the machine if a problem appears, and correct it before returning the machine to service. Record the replacement and inspection results in the maintenance log.

## Time estimate
Allow 2 hours for the Coolant valve replacement, cleanup, and verification. Actual time may vary with access, coolant handling, and the condition of the connections.
