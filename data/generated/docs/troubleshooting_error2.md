# Troubleshooting error2: Hydraulic pressure irregularity

## Symptoms
The error code **error2** indicates **Hydraulic pressure irregularity**. Treat it as a prompt to inspect the hydraulic system and the conditions around the alert, not as proof that a particular part has failed. Record when the code appears, whether it recurs, and what the machine was doing at the time. Note any visible leaks, unusual sounds, or changes in machine operation, and preserve relevant operating and maintenance records for comparison.

## Likely causes
Follow-up records show that **Hydraulic pump** was the component most often reported failed within seven days after error2, with 256 events. This makes the Hydraulic pump the leading component to inspect, but the follow-up count does not establish that the error caused the failure.

These are less frequent follow-ups than Hydraulic pump. Consider them when symptoms, inspection findings, or maintenance history point to them; do not replace components based on the counts alone.

## Troubleshooting steps
1. Follow site safety procedures and place the machine in a safe state before inspecting hydraulic equipment. Do not open or disconnect pressurized lines.
2. Record the error details and operating context. Check whether error2 is isolated or recurring, and preserve the associated sensor readings and machine logs.
3. Visually inspect accessible hydraulic lines, fittings, and connections for leaks, damage, or signs of looseness. Report findings and address hazards using approved procedures.
4. Inspect the Hydraulic pump for visible damage, abnormal sound, or other symptoms, and review its service history. Use the manufacturer’s approved diagnostic procedure before deciding whether repair is needed.
5. Check the coolant circuit and Coolant valve if the operating symptoms or inspection findings suggest a coolant-flow issue. Verify the valve using approved maintenance instructions.
6. If symptoms indicate mechanical or drive trouble, inspect the Spindle bearing and Drive motor according to their approved procedures. Avoid assuming either has failed solely because it appears in follow-up records.
7. After any confirmed issue is corrected, run the machine through an approved functional check and monitor for recurrence. Document the outcome and escalate unresolved or repeated error2 events to qualified maintenance personnel.
